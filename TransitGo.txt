TransitGo — Philippines flight and RoRo trip planner

OPENING THE APP
Open index.html in a modern browser. No build step, API key, or package
installation is required.

BOOKING SEARCH
Choose an origin, destination, departure date, traveler count, and either
Airplane or RoRo / Ferry. The app creates search links to 12Go and links to
airline and ferry operator websites. It does NOT fetch live fares, schedules,
seat inventory, sailings, or vehicle availability. The destination directory
is a curated static list in script.js, not a live database or verified list of
routes served by every provider. Provider websites are the source of truth.

REAL-TIME DATA
There is currently no TransitGo server-side database or operator data feed.
Saved trips use browser local storage on the visitor's device. Internet access
is needed for provider websites and WhatsApp, but a connection alone does not
make TransitGo's curated destination list or booking links real-time.
Real-time inventory requires an authorized API/feed from the relevant
airlines, ferry operators, or booking aggregator, plus permission to display
its data. Prices and capacity can change after any check; they cannot be
guaranteed accurate at all times.

HOTEL DISCOVERY
The hotel finder queries the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service for named
hotel, guest-house, hostel, motel, resort, or apartment map objects within
about 8 km of the selected destination. It needs internet access and no API key
or Google billing. Results are community-maintained, can be missing or out of
date, and are not a complete hotel directory. OpenStreetMap does NOT provide
nightly rates, date-specific room availability, or booking inventory. Confirm
details and current prices with a booking provider before making plans.

OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL). The
page displays attribution and links to the license. The public Overpass service
has usage limits and no availability guarantee; searches run only when the
visitor submits the form. Do not send repeated automated requests or treat the
results as guaranteed real-time hotel facts.

RORO VEHICLES
Choose a vehicle type to see it in the planner, then confirm the exact route,
vehicle space, dimensions, documents, passenger tickets, and total fare with
the ferry operator. Vehicle details are not automatically submitted to an
operator booking system.

BOOKING AND SAVED TRIPS
Bookings and payments take place on external provider websites. TransitGo
does not reserve seats or vehicles. Saved itineraries are reminders kept in
this browser and are not tickets or reservations.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT CHAT
The "Ask us" widget provides a payment FAQ and opens a WhatsApp conversation
with the TransitGo owner when a visitor sends a message. The owner replies
personally. Messages are not stored or answered automatically on this website.
The configured WhatsApp contact is published in the client-side app, so use a
business number intended for public customer inquiries.
